# B1 · 极简手绘风

Approved baseline: the original-speed Sadaltager **V15** sample, not the earlier minimalist line/PPT-like drafts. B1 is separate from all A-style generation prompts.

## Visual language

- Near-white paper (`#FDFDFC` in local rendering), very faint broad grid and restrained stable grain; no dark beige wash. Keep the background quiet.
- Expressive hand-drawn stick figures, lively eyes/brows/mouth and clear whole-body poses, black slightly irregular outlines. Use the approved original three-pose sheet for the included demo; design appropriate characters/references for other stories. Do not impose these three poses on an entire long film.
- Hand-drawn colored props, marker accents and curved arrows; limited warm yellow/red/green plus ink/navy. Real screenshots may appear briefly as evidence. Match roughness/line weight, avoiding polished UI cards mixed with crude symbols.
- Large, short in-scene text is the visual focal point. The original accepted demo used Chalkboard on macOS; the portable package includes OFL Caveat as a fallback, not a claim of pixel-identical typography. For other scripts/languages configure a readable licensed font with the required glyphs.
- At 1280×720, start captions at 34px, short phrase-based lines, bottom safe band and high readable contrast. Scale for other ratios/resolutions; verify on a phone. Avoid crowding text, faces or evidence into the caption band.

## Rhythm and sound

Voice leads. Retain natural pace and pauses; target duration is not permission to stretch speech. Character shots occupy roughly **60–70% of direct screen time** for the approved B1 promotional format, not 60–70% of image area and not a hard requirement for every possible story. Include character-free closeups and reading holds.

Use semantic match cuts, a prop carried between ideas, closeups and a settled frame after an action. Do not apply a repeating title/icon/underline build to every shot, speed up all cuts or keep the camera moving constantly. Local motion and restrained synchronized SFX should support the spoken beat. Keep the chosen voice fixed; Sadaltager is the demo choice, not a restriction on users.

## Acceptance

Inspect consistent identity, varied expressions, meaningful local motion, readable text, clear visual hierarchy, uncluttered compositions, original speech speed, subtitle timing and balanced SFX. Compare with the approved V15 sample rather than an unrelated A-style video. The public preview is the already approved V15 file; runtime fixtures are engineering evidence, not additional approved showcases.
